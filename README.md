# Thomas Pitz – Piano Works

A collection of original works for solo piano by **Thomas Pitz**.

The scores in this repository are publication-ready PDF editions from the 2026 piano collection. They are made available so that pianists can explore the music directly from the score.

## Scores

2. **Albumblatt für Anne**

4. **Nocturne**
5. **Prélude élégiaque in E minor**
6. **Birthday Valse for Lisa**
7. **Valse brillante**
8. **Rag**
9. **Capriccio**
10. **Jazz Toccata**
11. **Präludium**
12. **Impromptu**
13. **Herbstfantasie**
14. **Fantasie**
15. **Große Fantasie in E**

## Featured work

### Intermezzo for Piano

A short lyrical piano piece in a broadly classical tradition, with a contemporary harmonic language.

The *Intermezzo* is currently being shared as the first featured work from the collection.

## Use of the scores

The scores may be downloaded free of charge for personal study.
